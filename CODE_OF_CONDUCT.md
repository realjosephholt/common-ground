# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

This project is used by people organising in their own communities, sometimes at
personal risk. That raises the bar for how we treat each other.

**Expected:** good-faith engagement, specific and technical criticism, respect for
people's privacy and safety, and patience with newcomers.

**Not tolerated:** harassment of any kind; publishing others' private information;
sexualised language or imagery; sustained disruption; and advocacy of violence.

## Project-specific rules

- **Do not post real organising details in public issues.** Names, addresses, plans,
  and membership lists do not belong in a bug report. Use synthetic examples.
- **Do not use issues or PRs to campaign.** This repository is for building the tool.
  Disputes about specific political causes belong elsewhere.
- **Assume adversaries read this repository.** Discuss security weaknesses through the
  process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainers via a private security advisory on the
repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban any contributor.
