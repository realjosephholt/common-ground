# Common Ground

Finding causes with enough genuinely shared support that collective action is possible,
and then organising that action. This glossary is the project's canonical vocabulary —
if a term here has an `_Avoid_` list, those words are drift, not synonyms.

## Language

### Discovery

**Statement**:
A single concrete proposal, written by a participant, that people vote on. Always phrased
as an action with an intended outcome, never as a complaint.
_Avoid_: Proposal, post, idea, suggestion

**Conversation**:
A bounded deliberation space, scoped to a Region and a seed topic, within which
Statements are written and voted on.
_Avoid_: Forum, thread, discussion

**Region**:
The named administrative area a Conversation is scoped to — a county, city, district or
ward. Chosen because Targets hold jurisdiction over administrative areas, not over
arbitrary map cells.
_Avoid_: Area, locality, catchment, geo

**Vote**:
One participant's agree / disagree / pass response to one Statement.

**Opinion Group**:
A set of participants who vote alike, discovered from the vote matrix rather than
declared by anyone. A Conversation has between two and five.
_Avoid_: Faction, side, camp, cluster

**Cause**:
A group of related Statements that share actors, targets or policies, discovered from the
knowledge graph. The unit a person actually cares about; the Statement is the specific
ask inside it.
_Avoid_: Issue, topic, theme, movement

**Spearhead**:
The single highest-readiness Statement within a Cause. A Cause's readiness is its
Spearhead's readiness, because people organise around a specific ask rather than a theme.

**Readiness**:
How close a Statement is to being actionable, combining Bridge, Commitment, Density and
Concreteness such that a zero in any one of them is a zero overall.
_Avoid_: Score, support level, popularity

**Bridge**:
The lowest agreement a Statement achieves in any Opinion Group. High Bridge means even
the least enthusiastic group is on board.
_Avoid_: Consensus, approval

**Commitment**:
What a participant will actually contribute to a Statement — support, funds, turning up,
a skill, or organising. Distinct from agreeing with it.
_Avoid_: Pledge, sign-up, RSVP

**Density**:
How geographically concentrated a Statement's committed people are.

**Concreteness**:
Whether a Statement names a Target, a specific ask, and a deadline.

### Organising

**Co-sponsorship**:
The human act of endorsing a ready Statement for promotion. Readiness alone never creates
a Campaign; Co-sponsorship does.
_Avoid_: Approval, sign-off

**Campaign**:
Sustained organised effort to win one Statement's ask from one Target.

**Target**:
The specific decision-maker who could grant a Campaign's ask.
_Avoid_: Opponent, enemy, stakeholder

**Jurisdiction**:
The area a Target holds authority over. Often a Region, but not always — an elected
official's district frequently matches no administrative area, so a Jurisdiction may be
only a name.

**Tactic**:
A reusable kind of collective action — petition, public comment, rally, canvass, boycott,
mutual aid drive — carrying its own prerequisites and safety notes.

**Action**:
One scheduled instance of a Tactic: a time, a place, and roles to fill. Note that "direct
action" is the project's tagline and never a term in the model.
_Avoid_: Event, protest, direct action

**Role**:
A job that must be filled for an Action to run safely — marshal, legal observer, medic,
media liaison, driver, childcare.

**Escalation**:
Moving a Campaign's ask to a Target with authority over the previous one, when the first
refuses or does not respond. The Campaign continues; it does not become a new one.

**Outcome**:
The recorded result of an Action or Campaign against its Target, including whether the
Target conceded. Outcomes are what later Tactic choices are judged against.
_Avoid_: Result, retrospective, post-mortem

### Trust and safety

**Participant**:
A person taking part in a Conversation. Pseudonymous by default; a real name is never
required.
_Avoid_: User, member, account

**Verification Level**:
How strongly a Participant's distinctness from other Participants is established. Used to
gate entry to a Conversation, never to make one person's Vote count more than another's.

**Vouch**:
One Participant's assertion that another is a distinct real person.

**Report**:
A Participant's flag that content breaches the content policy.

**Redaction**:
Removal of a Statement's text at its author's request, leaving the Statement itself and
the Votes cast on it intact. Always recorded in the Moderation Log.

**Moderation Log**:
The public, append-only record of every moderation action taken on an instance.

**Instance**:
One deployment of Common Ground, with its own Participants and data.
_Avoid_: Server, tenant, site
